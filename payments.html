Payments & refunds

Paying for events

Event fees can be paid in the app by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or directly to the society by bank transfer. In-app payments are processed securely by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.

Cancellations and refunds

If you can't make an event, let the committee know as soon as possible. If you cancel before we've paid the course, you'll get a full refund. After that, a refund is at the committee's discretion — for example if someone takes your place.

In-app payments are refunded to the card you paid with, and usually arrive within 5–10 working days. Other payments are refunded by bank transfer.

If an event is cancelled by the society

Everyone who paid gets a full refund.
